# The mission is general terminal confidence, not three separate tracks

Clarified through the opening questions: although GitHub, the Linux file system, and Synology SSH are the three contexts that motivate the user, the underlying goal is **general command-line confidence** — being unafraid of any terminal. The three contexts are where that fear shows up, not three separate curricula. Recorded in [[MISSION]].

**Implication:** teach the shared foundation once and reuse it across contexts (e.g. the navigation commands from Lesson 0001 reappear over SSH in Lesson 0002), rather than running three parallel tracks.
