# Using This Workspace in Obsidian

This whole repo doubles as an **Obsidian vault** — a linked graph of your learning. Obsidian is free and works on desktop, iPhone, and iPad.

## Open it
1. Install Obsidian.
2. Choose **Open folder as vault** and pick the `LearnCode` folder.
3. Open [[KNOWLEDGE-MAP]] — that's your home note. Everything links out from there.
4. Open **Graph View** (the connected-dots icon) to see your notes as a web.

## What forms the graph
The markdown files are the linked notes:
- [[MISSION]], [[RESOURCES]], [[NOTES]] — the compass
- `learning-records/` — one note per genuine insight, each linking back to [[MISSION]] and friends. Backlinks appear automatically in Obsidian.
- [[KNOWLEDGE-MAP]] — the hub (a "Map of Content") that ties it all together

As we add learning records, the graph grows on its own.

## Lessons & cheat sheets
These are HTML (so they render beautifully with the dark-mode toggle). Obsidian links to them as normal links from [[KNOWLEDGE-MAP]] — click to open in a browser. On iPhone, the [GitHub Pages site](https://syncopatedsyntax.github.io/LearnCode/) is the easiest way to read them.

## Keeping your own notes
When something clicks, make your own note anywhere in the vault and link it with `[[double brackets]]`. Tell me and I'll fold it into the format and update [[KNOWLEDGE-MAP]] so nothing gets orphaned.

## Syncing across devices
The repo lives on GitHub. To keep the vault in sync on your phone, either use Obsidian's own Sync, or a community **Git** plugin that pulls/commits this repo automatically. Tell me which you'd like and I'll walk you through it.
