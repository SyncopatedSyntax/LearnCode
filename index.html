Linux Command Line Confidence

Beginner-friendly terminal skills for GitHub, general Linux, and SSH into your Synology NAS.

Lessons

  • 0001 — Finding Your Way Around — pwd, ls, cd, and absolute vs. relative paths
  • 0002 — SSH Into Your NAS — connect, look around safely, and disconnect
  • 0003 — Reading Files Without Changing Them — cat, less, head, tail, and their name origins
  • 0004 — Finding Things — find (files by name) and grep (text inside files)
  • 0005 — Getting Help Yourself — --help, man, and finding a command by what it does
  • 0006 — Making & Changing Files — mkdir, touch, cp, mv, rm, and staying safe
  • 0007 — Pipes & Redirection — >, >>, |, and snapping commands together
  • 0008 — Understanding Permissions — decode rwx, "permission denied," chmod, and what sudo is
